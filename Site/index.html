    About WhenScape

    WhenScape shows the light, sky and weather at a place and time: the Sun and Moon where they are, clouds, rain or snow, and the day’s Sun path over a 3D model of the place.

    Using it with your calendar

    Copy an event in your calendar app and press ⌘V here (or use Paste event) to bring in its date and time, then choose where it takes place. Copy link makes a link to the current place and time that you can paste into an event’s URL or notes.

    Google Maps

    WhenScape includes Google Maps features and content: the map, place search and the 3D model. Your use of them is subject to the Google Maps Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

    Privacy

    Saved places, recent places and settings stay in this browser. Place searches, the map and the 3D model come from Google, which receives your searches and the places you look at. Weather comes from Open-Meteo, which receives the place’s coordinates and the time. Your own location is read only when you choose Here, with your browser’s permission. Pasted calendar text is read in this page and not sent anywhere. Links keep the place and time after the “#”, which browsers do not send to this site.

    Data

    Astronomy is calculated. Weather uses forecasts, historical estimates or a five-year seasonal baseline from Open-Meteo (free for non-commercial use). The 3D model was photographed in daylight and is relit approximately; it shows the place as captured, not as it was at a past date. Rendering by CesiumJS.
